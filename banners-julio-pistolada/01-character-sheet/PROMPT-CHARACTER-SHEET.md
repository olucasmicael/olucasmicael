## PROMPT 1.0 — TRAVA DE IDENTIDADE

Cole este bloco no começo de **todo** prompt: ficha, figurino ou cena. Logo depois dele, cole o **ESTILO 3D** (Prompt 0).

```
CHARACTER LOCK — JULIO PISTOLADA. The attached photos are the ONLY reference for this person's identity. Reproduce him exactly:
- Face: round, full face with wide cheeks; warm light-brown/tan skin (#A17157 lit, #A97150 shadow); full lips.
- Hair: short black tightly-curled hair, skin fade on the sides, slightly taller on top.
- Beard: thick full black beard (#333130), fuller and longer at the chin, sharp lined cheek edge, connected to the mustache.
- Body: heavyset, broad, stocky, plus-size build with wide shoulders and thick arms. NEVER slim him down, NEVER make him muscular or lean.
- Signature item (always on his face): silver metal wraparound sport sunglasses with oval ruby-red mirrored lenses (#CF394A lens, #95908A frame), "Juliet" style.
- Signature accessories: large black-and-silver chronograph watch on the left wrist, thin silver chain.
- Attitude: serious and confident, chin up. Signature poses: arms crossed over the chest, or a "finger gun" hand gesture pointing up next to his face.
- CLOTHING: IGNORE the clothes in the reference photos completely. NEVER draw the black jersey with the crest, the "NARIZES FC" lettering, the brand logo or the red-and-blue sleeve band from the photos. Use ONLY the outfit described in this prompt.
Do not change his ethnicity, age (mid-twenties), face shape, beard shape or body type. No tattoos, earrings or hats unless the prompt asks.
```

## PROMPT 0 — ESTILO 3D GTA (o “visual de videogame”)

Os prompts antigos diziam “arte promocional pintada, com contorno”, e por isso saía **cartoon**. O que vocês querem é o **personagem 3D de dentro do jogo**, como print de cutscene ou do modo foto. Cole este bloco **logo depois da trava de identidade**, em todo prompt.

**Opção A — “GTA V 3D”** (cara de jogo mais evidente, recomendado pra ficha):
```
STYLE — 3D VIDEO GAME CHARACTER: render him as a real-time 3D character model from a modern open-world crime video game, exactly like Grand Theft Auto V running on PC with max graphics (Rockstar RAGE engine look). Realistic human proportions, game-quality 3D skin with pores and subsurface scattering, 3D modeled curly hair cards, realistic beard, PBR fabric with folds and stitching, real-time ray-traced lighting and soft shadows, slight game-engine sharpness. It must look like a SCREENSHOT FROM THE GAME / a character model viewer, NOT a drawing.
NEGATIVE: no cartoon, no comic, no illustration, no painting, no cel shading, no black outlines, no anime, no caricature, no exaggerated big head, no vector art, no flat colors.
```

**Opção B — “GTA VI cinematográfico”** (quase foto, igual aos trailers; recomendado pros banners):
```
STYLE — NEXT-GEN 3D CUTSCENE: a still frame from a next-gen open-world crime game cinematic trailer in the style of Grand Theft Auto VI — near-photorealistic real-time 3D rendering, ray-traced global illumination and reflections, volumetric light, cinematic depth of field, subtle film grain and lens flare, Vice City / Florida color grading (warm sunset orange, hot pink, purple). Realistic 3D game character, NOT a real photograph, NOT a drawing.
NEGATIVE: no cartoon, no comic, no illustration, no painting, no cel shading, no outlines, no caricature, no vector art.
```

**Dica:** se ainda sair desenhado, escreva no começo do prompt `3D game screenshot, Unreal/RAGE engine render` e peça “make it look like an in-game GTA screenshot, not fan art”.

## Depois de gerar

1. Gere de 4 a 8 opções do **Prompt 1.1** e escolha a mais parecida com o Julio. Mostre pra ele e confirme.

3. Daqui pra frente, anexe a **ficha aprovada + `01-rosto-frente-oculos-vermelho.png`** em todo pedido das etapas 2 e 3.

## Se o rosto “fugir”

- **Emagreceu:** repita “heavyset, plus-size — do not slim him down” e anexe `04-corpo-inteiro-em-pe.png`.
- **Barba mudou:** anexe `09-detalhe-barba.png` e escreva “beard exactly like the reference”.
- **Saiu com a camisa do Narizes das fotos:** repita a linha `CLOTHING: IGNORE the clothes in the reference photos` e descreva a roupa nova com mais detalhe. Se continuar, anexe só os recortes de rosto (`01`, `03`, `08`, `09`) e tire as fotos de corpo.
- **Óculos trocou:** anexe `08-detalhe-oculos.png`.
- **Pele clareou ou escureceu:** cite os códigos de cor. Eles foram medidos nas fotos e são aproximados, porque a luz muda o tom.
